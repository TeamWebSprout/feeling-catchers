Stay in the room.

This app is a prop for you and your child together, not a babysitter. The research on handing a device to a distressed young child is not encouraging. What works is co-regulation: your calm nervous system next to theirs.

Breathe along out loud.

When the creature breathes, you breathe. Audibly. Your child will copy you long before they copy a cartoon.

The real goal is practice on calm days.

Creatures only grow when you play during good moods. A skill rehearsed at 8pm Tuesday is the skill available at meltdown o'clock.

Put this on your Home Screen

It launches full screen and works with no signal. It also protects the data: Safari can clear a website's saved data after seven days of not visiting it, and Home Screen apps are exempt from that rule. Over a two-week test, that is the difference between keeping your pals and losing them.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (a square with an arrow pointing up)
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add

This only works in Safari. Chrome and Firefox on iPhone cannot install web apps.
